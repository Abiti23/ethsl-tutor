What is EthSL Tutor?

A small learning helper for hearing family members in Ethiopia who want to communicate with their Deaf loved ones. It teaches everyday words in English and Amharic, quizzes you, and lets you practice hand shapes with your camera.

Built by

Mintesnot Mathewos Mentamo, Hawassa, Ethiopia — founder of EthSL.

An honest note

Sign language is visual and varies by community. This app teaches words and hand-shape practice, but it cannot replace learning from a Deaf signer. Please verify every sign with a Deaf teacher in your community.

Privacy

The camera practice runs 100% on your device. No video is recorded, uploaded, or shared.
